## 1. Executive Summary

This report satisfies **MAS FEAT Transparency Principle T1** (Decision Explainability) for the CAGE Governed Financial Advisor deployed in the APAC_MAS region.

MAS FEAT T1 requires that AI systems used in financial services be able to explain their decisions to:
- **Customers** — in plain language, at the time of decision
- **Regulators (MAS)** — in technical detail, on request

This report describes how CAGE achieves T1 compliance through its explainability chain, audit record reconstruction capability, and human oversight mechanisms.

## 3. Decision-Making Process

### 3.1 Pipeline Overview

Every customer-facing financial recommendation passes through the following stages in order:

```
Customer Request
       │
       ▼
1. [NeMo Guardrails Input Gate]
   - Screens for harmful, toxic, or injection content
   - Blocks requests that violate content policy (A.5.2)
       │
       ▼
2. [OPA Policy Engine — Trade Governance]
   - RBAC check: trader role limits (junior ≤ S$5,000, senior ≤ S$500,000)
   - Token quota check: fail-closed if inference budget exhausted
   - Returns: ALLOW / MANUAL_REVIEW / DENY / GOVERNANCE_VIOLATION
       │
       ▼
3. [DoWhy Causal Gatekeeper]
   - Counterfactual fairness analysis: would a different demographic group
     receive a materially different recommendation?
   - Confidence score computation (range: 0.0–1.0)
       │
       ▼
4. [HITL Gate — Human-in-the-Loop]
   - Triggered when: confidence < 0.95 OR amount > S$10,000
   - SLA: 1 hour (APAC_MAS)
   - Human operator reviews and approves/overrides
       │
       ▼
5. [Explainer Agent]
   - Generates plain-language explanation of the recommendation
   - Records decision rationale in WORM UCA record
       │
       ▼
6. [NeMo Guardrails Output Rail]
   - Screens AI output for harmful content, PII leakage
   - Final gate before customer delivery
       │
       ▼
Customer-Facing Response + Explanation
```

### 3.2 Decision Factors

For every financial recommendation, the following factors are documented in the WORM UCA audit record:

| Factor | Description | Customer-Visible? |
|---|---|---|
| OPA policy outcome | ALLOW / MANUAL_REVIEW / DENY | No (internal control) |
| Confidence score | Model's self-assessed certainty (0.0–1.0) | On request |
| Causal fairness check | Whether recommendation is fair across demographic groups | On request |
| HITL operator decision | If human review was triggered, operator rationale | Yes |
| Explainer output | Plain-language recommendation rationale | Yes — delivered at time of recommendation |
| NeMo guardrail status | Whether content was flagged for policy compliance | No (internal control) |

## 4. Customer Explainability (MAS FEAT T1)

### 4.1 At-Time-of-Recommendation Explanation

Every recommendation delivered to a customer includes:
1. **Plain-language recommendation:** Generated by the `explainer` LangGraph node
2. **Confidence indicator:** Simple confidence band (High / Medium / Requires human review)
3. **Key factors:** Top 3 factors that influenced the recommendation (derived from causal gatekeeper analysis)

**Example customer explanation:**
> "This recommendation is based on your declared risk profile (moderate), current portfolio allocation (40% equities, 60% bonds), and current market conditions. Our AI system has high confidence in this recommendation. This recommendation was reviewed by a compliance officer before delivery."

### 4.2 Customer Right to Explanation (PDPA / MAS FEAT)

Customers may request a detailed explanation of any recommendation. The explanation will include:
- The key factors that influenced the recommendation
- Whether human review was involved
- The regulatory basis for any restrictions applied

**Process:** Customer contacts [TBD — customer service channel]. Response within 10 business days.

## 5. Regulatory Explainability (MAS Notice 655 §10)

### 5.1 WORM UCA Record Reconstruction

Every governance decision produces a **WORM (Write-Once Read-Many) UCA audit record** that enables complete reconstruction of the decision for MAS on-demand audit.

The UCA record includes:
- Timestamp (nanosecond precision)
- Request context hash (SHA-256 chain)
- OPA policy input and outcome
- DoWhy causal confidence score and counterfactual analysis
- HITL escalation details (if triggered)
- KMS governance signature (HSM-backed, asymmetric)

**Retention:** 7 years (MAS FEAT A2; `docs/AUDIT_LOG_RETENTION_SCHEDULE.md`)

### 5.2 On-Demand MAS Audit Access

For MAS supervisory access, the following data is available on request:
1. **Langfuse APAC_MAS compliance project** — full OTel trace timeline for any request
2. **GCS WORM UCA records** — `gs://$CAGE_AUDIT_BUCKET_APAC_MAS/uca-records/`
3. **Lula compliance assessment results** — automated compliance validation runs

### 6.2 Model Limitations

Customers are informed that:
- The AI system makes **recommendations**, not binding financial decisions
- All high-value recommendations (> S$10,000) are reviewed by a human operator
- The AI system may have limitations in unusual market conditions or novel financial scenarios
- Historical performance of AI recommendations does not guarantee future results
