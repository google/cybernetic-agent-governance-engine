# Universal AI Risk Taxonomy

**Document ID:** TAX-UNIV-AIR-001  
**Frameworks:** NIST AI 100-1 / AI 600-1, ISO/IEC 22989, ISO/IEC 42001 §A.5  
**Assurance Posture:** ILLUSTRATIVE_REFERENCE  
**Status:** Active  

## 1. Purpose
Defines normalized risk categories and consequence impact tiers used across all CAGE governance policies, Rego bundles, and CBF admissibility sets.

## 2. Risk Dimensions
1. **Safety & Physical Actuation (HAZ-01):** Direct physical impact or unauthorized actuator control violating safety invariants.
2. **Financial & Contractual Exposure (HAZ-02):** Unchecked portfolio allocation, transaction execution exceeding capital ceilings, or binding commitments made without quorum authorization.
3. **Information Disclosure & Sovereignty (HAZ-03):** Jurisdictional spillover, cross-region data transfer violations, or unsealed telemetry leakage.
4. **Agentic Drift & Goal Misalignment (HAZ-04):** Hallucinated tool invocation, cycle loops in agent graphs, or subversion of policy constraints.
